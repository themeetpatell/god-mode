# Example 06 — UAE GTM (90 days)

**Goal:** `Plan our UAE GTM for B2B finance SaaS — 90 days`

(Requires `pack-founder-uae` installed.)

## Expected roadmap

```
GOAL: 90-day UAE GTM plan for B2B finance SaaS targeting UAE SMB FOps leads.
ASSUMPTIONS: AED pricing; warm-intro motion + founder content; minimal paid in first 30 days.

Phase 1: Region-localized ICP + offer  [sequential]
  T1.1  Localize ICP + buying triggers   → Opus   | est: 1500
  T1.2  Localize offer + pricing (AED)   → Sonnet | est: 800   | needs: T1.1

Phase 2: Channel design                [sequential]
  T2.1  Pick 2 channels for the region   → Opus   | est: 1200  | needs: T1.1
  T2.2  Warm-intro engine plan           → Sonnet | est: 1000  | needs: T2.1
  T2.3  Partner ecosystem candidates     → Sonnet | est: 800   | needs: T2.1

Phase 3: Funnel math                   [parallel]
  T3.1  Funnel + CAC math                → Sonnet | est: 1200  | needs: T2.*
  T3.2  Lifecycle automation map         → Sonnet | est: 1000

Phase 4: Execution plan                [sequential]
  T4.1  30/60/90 plan with kill criteria → Sonnet | est: 1500  | needs: T3.*
  T4.2  Hire trigger + spend ceiling     → Opus   | est: 600   | needs: T4.1

EST. TOTAL OUTPUT TOKENS: ~9600
```

## What gets shipped

A complete `growth-engine` brief layered with `uae-gtm-strategist` regional knowledge:
- ICP localized for UAE SMB FOps reality (not generic B2B)
- AED pricing with VAT/CT considerations
- Channels weighted for warm-intro + founder content (not paid-LinkedIn-first)
- WhatsApp + community sequences (per `whatsapp-sales-script` + `community-distribution-playbook`)
- Compliance notes (PDPL, e-invoicing)
- 30/60/90 with falsifiable metrics + kill criteria

## What you'd ask differently

- Replace "B2B finance SaaS" with your category
- Add "we have AED 50K budget for 90 days" — sharpens the math
- Add "we're pre-revenue" or "we have 12 customers" — changes the channel mix
- "Add KSA in month 3" — adds Phase 5 for regional expansion
