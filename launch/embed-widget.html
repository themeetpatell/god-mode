Host page — your product, your content, your app

This is a demo of the God Mode embeddable widget. It floats in the bottom-right. Drop it on any web page and your users can run AI orchestration without leaving your product.

In v1.4 this is a scaffold UI. In v1.5 it connects to either:

  1. Self-hosted webhook ingress — embed config points at your /v1/run server
  2. Hosted SaaS — embed config points at the marketplace's hosted endpoint

Embedding

<script src="https://cdn.themeetpatel.dev/widget/v1.js"></script>
<script>
  GodMode.init({
    server_url: "https://your-ingress.example.com",
    token: "",
    default_goal_placeholder: "What can I help you ship?",
    theme: "dark"
  });
</script>
