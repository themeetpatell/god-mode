# Community Distribution Playbook (UAE/GCC)

In the UAE, the bidirectional truth: warm intros close 4× faster than cold, and the warm-intro engine is the community you build presence in. This skill is the founder's manual.

## When to use

- Early-stage UAE GTM where outbound + paid won't scale yet
- B2B SaaS where the buyer is an owner/operator who lives in WhatsApp + events
- NRI segment plays (Indian-Emirati business community is dense + connected)
- Family-office adjacent products (private network access matters)
- Re-entering a market where cold motion stalled

## The 5 community surfaces

| Surface | Who's there | Density | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp groups** | SMB owners, expat professionals, vertical communities | Very high | Medium (need to be invited, behave well) |
| **Founder dinners** | Funded + bootstrap founders | High | High to host, low to attend |
| **Industry meetups** | Vertical practitioners | Medium | Low (just show up) |
| **Major events (GITEX/STEP/LEAP)** | Everyone, condensed | Extremely high during the week | Very high (booth) or low (walk floor) |
| **Family-office networks** | UHNW, single/multi-family offices | Low # but high value | High (relationship-mediated, slow) |

Pick 2 to dominate, not 5 to half-do.

## WhatsApp groups (the highest-leverage move most founders skip)

The UAE runs on WhatsApp groups. There's a group for every niche: fintech founders, SaaS founders, NRI CFOs, e-commerce ops, accounting firm owners, IT directors, expat-by-school-district, neighborhood-by-emirate.

How to enter:
1. **Audit your existing network.** Who's in groups already? Ask for an intro.
2. **Build a "give first" reputation.** Answer 3 questions in the group before pitching anything.
3. **Never broadcast.** Mass-pitching in a group = removed + reputation hit.
4. **DM, don't post.** When you see a question that fits your product, DM the asker.
5. **Run your own group only if you have unique value to convene around.** Most founder-run groups die.

Group hygiene (if you join):
- Read 20-30 messages before posting
- Introduce yourself with one line
- Add value 3-5 times before any soft mention of your work
- Never link-drop without context

Group hygiene (if you run one):
- < 200 members ideally; closeness > scale
- One topic, enforced
- Pin rules + intros
- Weekly thread to keep it alive
- Quietly remove broadcasters

## Founder dinners (the highest-trust channel)

Format that works in Dubai:
- 8-12 people, one table
- One unifying theme ("UAE B2B founders Q2", "fintech operators in MENA")
- Light moderation by you (intros, one question per person)
- Phones away during dinner
- Hosted at a known venue (private dining at <restaurant>)

Cost: AED 2-4K per dinner. Hit rate on warm intros: 1-3 useful relationships per dinner.

Rotate. Don't invite the same crowd to back-to-back dinners.

## Events — the GCC big four

| Event | When | What it's good for |
|---|---|---|
| **GITEX (Dubai)** | October | Tech buyers, government, enterprise. Boothing: expensive. Walking: free + high-leverage if you target meetings. |
| **STEP (Dubai)** | March | Startup-focused. Better for raising / partnerships than direct enterprise sales. |
| **LEAP (Riyadh)** | February | KSA enterprise + government. Huge if KSA is on your roadmap. |
| **Money 20-20 / Dubai FinTech Summit** | May | Fintech-specific. Money flows. |
| **Cityscape / The Hotel Show / WETEX** | varies | Vertical events — best if your buyer attends one of these specifically. |

Playbook for events you don't booth:
- 4 weeks out: identify 20-30 named meetings you want
- LinkedIn message: "in town for GITEX, would love 15 min Oct 14-16"
- 1 week out: confirm with each
- During: 6-8 meetings/day, lunch as the anchor
- Day after: send notes + next step within 24 hours

ROI: a single AED 4K event trip with 12 booked meetings can beat AED 40K paid spend.

## Family-office / UHNW networks

Different game. Not "I'll DM them on LinkedIn." It's:
- One trusted intermediary (lawyer, banker, advisor, or another principal)
- Multiple introductions over months
- Product / service that respects their time + privacy
- No marketing-style follow-up — slow, polite, value-first

Only worth pursuing if your average deal size is > AED 100K and the buyer profile genuinely is principal-level.

## NRI segment specifics

The Indian-Emirati business community is dense (~3.5M+), connected, and underserved by generic GTM. Surfaces:

- WhatsApp groups by:
  - Profession (CAs, doctors, IT, FOps)
  - State of origin (Gujaratis, Keralites, Punjabis — each have their networks)
  - Schools / colleges (alumni groups)
  - Apartment buildings (real WhatsApp groups exist)
- Indian-Emirati associations (e.g., IBPC, India Club, regional clubs)
- Indian publication ecosystem (Khaleej Times India desk, Gulf News, Al Bayan if Arabic-bilingual)
- Indian-friendly venues (Bombay Brasserie / Trèsind Studio circuits)

Cultural notes:
- Mixed Hindi-English in casual; English in formal
- Diwali / Holi / Eid / Christmas all observed; plan around
- Family-business decision dynamics — the senior may decide but the junior may evaluate

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Joining 20 WhatsApp groups and posting your link in each
- ❌ Booking a GITEX booth at AED 80K with no pre-event meeting list
- ❌ Hosting a "founder dinner" that's actually a sales pitch in a private room
- ❌ Treating "community-led" as a brand exercise instead of a measured channel
- ❌ Ignoring the NRI segment because you don't have a Hindi-speaker on the team (they speak English; you don't need Hindi to start)
- ❌ Asking a busy operator for "30 min to pick your brain" — no one has the time
- ❌ Showing up at one event and giving up
