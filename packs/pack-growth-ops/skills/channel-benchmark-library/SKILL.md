# Channel Benchmark Library

These are anchors a growth lead can use to (1) sanity-check a campaign's performance, (2) size a new channel before spending, and (3) push back on vendors over-promising results.

## How to use these numbers

- These are 50th-percentile bands from observed B2B SaaS / services in 2024-2026
- Your actuals can be 2× better or 2× worse depending on offer + ICP fit
- If your number is at the bottom of the band, the channel may not be a fit — diagnose before doubling down
- If your number is at the top of the band, you have a moat — invest more

## How to use this in a brief

When proposing a channel:

```
Channel: <channel>
Expected CAC: <benchmark range> from this library
Our advantage / disadvantage vs benchmark:
  - <thing that makes us better/worse than median>
Sizing:
  - At <spend> we'd expect <volume> at <CAC>
  - Break-even at <ACV vs payback>
First experiment to validate:
  - <small test>
  - Kill if CAC > $<n> after <m> meetings
```

When diagnosing why a channel underperforms:

```
Channel: <channel>
Actual CAC: $<n>
Library benchmark: $<range>
Position: <bottom / median / top> of band
Probable cause if at bottom:
  - <ICP mismatch / offer mismatch / creative mismatch / landing page>
Next step: <specific>
```

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Quoting these as facts when they're anchors
- ❌ Comparing your CAC to "industry CAC" without matching ICP / region / channel mix
- ❌ Doubling spend on a channel before you have ≥100 conversions to read
- ❌ Ignoring payback because LTV "could be higher"
- ❌ Treating benchmarks as targets ("our CAC is at benchmark, we're done") — being at median is mediocrity
- ❌ Using benchmarks from a different era (2020 CAC bears no relation to 2025 CAC for most channels)

## Updates

These numbers drift. Re-validate quarterly. When you observe a channel-wide shift in your own data + your peer network's data, update this skill.

## Verification

The verifier (class: data) will check that every benchmark cited in a brief uses a range, not a point estimate, and that the user's actuals are compared against the range with a stated position (bottom / median / top).
