# Deep Research

## Trigger
Use when the task needs current facts, source quality, comparisons, or market signal.

## Workflow
1. Define the research question and decision the research supports.
2. Gather primary sources first, then credible secondary sources.
3. Separate fact, inference, opinion, and uncertainty.
4. Synthesize into an operator-ready brief.
5. End with open questions and next research moves.

## Output
```
RESEARCH QUESTION:
DECISION SUPPORTED:
TOP FINDINGS:
SOURCE MAP:
IMPLICATIONS:
GAPS:
NEXT MOVES:
```

## Routing
- Haiku: source extraction, table cleanup.
- Sonnet: normal research synthesis.
- Opus: strategic conclusions, regulatory/compliance-sensitive calls, high-stakes market bets.
