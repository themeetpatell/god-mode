# Growth Engine

## Workflow
1. Define ICP, pain, offer, urgency, and buying trigger.
2. Map channels by intent level and speed-to-revenue.
3. Build funnel from awareness to advocacy.
4. Add attribution, enrichment, lead scoring, and lifecycle automation.
5. Design 30/60/90-day experiments.

## Rules
- Prefer compounding assets over one-off campaigns.
- Every channel must have tracking and conversion logic.
